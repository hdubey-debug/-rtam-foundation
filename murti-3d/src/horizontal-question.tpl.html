Why not a GLB?

An image-to-3D GLB is one baked mesh — no “cup circle” exists inside it to resize; stretching a radial band smears petals and suns. This study is built from the measured rings themselves, so every diameter moves cleanly and the counts never blur. Send your GLB anyway: its actual ring measures can be extracted and loaded here as a preset for comparison.
