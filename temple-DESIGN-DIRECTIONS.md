## 1 · The thesis

**The temple is a large version of the object inside it.** Six pairings:
basin → podium (its twelve rim-suns held for the entrance study) · lower petal
ring → ground floor · upper petal ring → gallery floor · **linga → shikhara**
(form and position only; the tower is never black) · drop → kalasha (the bindu)
· the nālī's direction → the entrance (the building's literal water-break is
the praṇāla). Generative, not literal: the real murti completes the system.

**The laws** (each earned by killing a wrong draft — see §5):
1. One instantiation per scale (the icon never stamps as pattern).
2. Counts live on compact, centred things — lintel, disc, ring, path; stretched
   surfaces carry only grammar.
3. The system's numbers never ride a repeating element.
4. One nakashi language — plainness reads as ashram, mixed motifs read as
   noise. Active exterior language (v3.3, founder's revision): ONE boss family
   at three scales + precise moldings. The symbolic alphabet (petal, sun disc,
   drop, screen) is RESERVED — the exterior never commits a profile the murti's
   own 3D design has not yet set.
5. The lineage outranks the codex (D1).

## 2 · The exterior

**2.1 The front — the program** *(render pending)*. Founder's massing —
colonnade, axial stair with cheek walls, double roof, tall tower from its own
base — wearing the kit. Composed, not counted: windows as the rhythm needs,
openings stacked over openings. The centre axis reads: steps → door → tower → kalasha.

**2.2 The podium** *(direction set)*. Five cast courses in flamed grey granite
cladding on the RC plinth: upāna ~150 rough foot · jagatī ~300 plain · kumuda
~150 **smooth torus** (petal roll reserved) · kaṇṭha ~150 **pearl course** · kapota ~150 cap + drip.
Why five: sit / rise / turn / speak (the recess stays dry — geometry protects
carving) / shed. Sources: Mayamata/Mānasāra pādabandha; Nagara vedibandha;
names per the sthapati. Stair: full-width axial, ~6 plain risers, chandraśilā;
12 walk stations per circuit on the terrace; praṇāla = the one break → the
square pool. Falls 1:80, joints in the shadow grooves.

**2.3 The columns** *(approved — founder-locked)*. Square, bold, plain: plinth,
shaft, neck, broad bracket + abacus. No counted carving on any column, ever.

**2.4 The nakashi language** *(revised & set — v3.3)*. Principle: temple
richness through repeated bosses, precise moldings, approved window frames and
strong structural members — NOT five symbolic shapes throughout. One family of
shallow hemispherical dome bosses (architectural studs in carved registers,
never loose beads) at three scales: beam course Ø~90@~180 deepest, framed by
steps/grooves; kaṇṭha course Ø~60@~120 dense/shallow in the recess; cornice
course Ø~50@~110 smallest beneath plain corona + drip. Podium torus stays
SMOOTH. Petal/sun/drop/screen = reserved tray, pending interior & entrance
studies. Grammar at junctions, never mid-wall. Plate: artifact Fig 5.

**2.5 The windows & the entrance** *(windows approved; entrance study open)*.
Windows LOCKED as rendered — count, placement, size, frame, head, sill —
standardized as one template (artifact Fig 6): head cap + drip, dentil blocks,
architrave step, frame ~140, molded sill on corbels; jali = separate precision
study. Entrance: the twelve-sun lintel row and pierced screen are NOT approved
— removed from the active design and held as study material (artifact Fig 7);
the entrance's iconography and counted moment resolve with the lineage (D1);
no count relocated merely to preserve it. Fixed now: teak double door
~1.8×3.4m, three stepped jambs, plain stepped head, chandraśilā, bronze
hardware. Sun made real: east door → equinox sunrise (D2); optional gnomon +
noon meridian line.

**2.6 The roofs & the crown** *(open — D4)*. Two tiers, crisp eaves, gutters,
flashed tower curb. Material per D4 study: natural copper accepted-as-aging, or
an honest dark seam roof; current orange tiles are placeholder. Tower rises
from a visible base band, masonry/RC to ground, seismic separation; 24-rib
amalaka (the exterior's one 24); kalasha gilded only if sanctioned; plain
saffron dhvaja, emblem per the lineage.

**2.7 The back & the sides** *(not started)*. After the front locks: praṇāla
elevation + pool, side rhythm in the same grammar, service openings as quiet
subordinate necessities.

**2.8 Materials & construction** *(direction set)*. Brick + RC = the Roman
method (plastered brick-faced concrete) + Indian brick lineage (Bhitargaon,
Bengal). Lime plaster + lime wash (never acrylic); run/precast moldings from
full-size profiles; drip under every projection; joints hidden in grooves.
~40 carved stone/best-precast inserts carry the counted pieces. Granite at
touch zones. Mahākāla reserved for sanctum + murti. Night register: twelve
hand-lit dīpas on observance nights + one soft kalasha — flame, never LED.

**2.9 Dimensions** *(needs founder input)*: plot + orientation (D2), footprint
(keep near-square), floor heights, podium ~1.1m, tower height target, sanctum
size (sthapati + murti), capacity for festival ops.
