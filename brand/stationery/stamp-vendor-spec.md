# मुहर — stamp-maker specification

*One page for the stamp shop. Artwork files travel with it.*

## 1 · Round seal, full chakra (गोल मुहर) — RATIFIED, this one goes to die

| Item | Specification |
|---|---|
| Artwork | `dist/seal-chakra-round.pdf` (true size) or `../dist/outlined/icons/rtam-seal-chakra-round.svg` |
| Die | **Ø 50 mm**, self-inking — Trodat **46050** or Colop **R50** |
| Process | Laser-engraved rubber, deep relief (≥ 1.1 mm) |
| Ink pad | **VIOLET** (IS 393 stamp-pad violet) |
| Artwork floors met | Thinnest negative channel (petal seams) **0.69 mm** · window dots Ø 1.76 mm · rim text base 2.64 mm · separator ring 0.59 mm |
| ⚠ Nukta dots | The dots under **ड़** (पहाड़ीखेड़ा) are ≈ 0.2 mm — **thicken to ≥ 0.4 mm** on the die. Nothing else may be altered |
| Note | This is the THICKENED STAMP EDITION of the temple's icon, drawn for rubber — never substitute the screen artwork, whose seams are 0.34 mm and will clog |
