exp/rta-chakra v2 — twelve true lotus petals, five faithful crafts

Your three rulings, applied. No mixing: the synthesis iteration is reverted; each candidate is itself again, keeping its round-one number (5 stays retired). Twelve from above: one corolla, and the old two-tier originals are preserved untouched in v1-24petal/ next door. Lotus, not rose: the petal is redrawn — it enters broad at the base, carries its belly low, and tapers long into a sharp ogival point — and the podium is rebalanced so twelve fill what twenty-four used to (tips reach .70 R; every other radius stays on the grid). Gold untouched: the Shivalinga always; the Adityas only in 6.
