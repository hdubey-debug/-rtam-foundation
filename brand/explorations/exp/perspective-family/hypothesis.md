# exp/perspective-family — hypothesis (Direction B)

**Claim.** The identity is not one mark but one OBJECT seen from three
vantages — exactly as the murti was engineered. Each projection maps to an
entity and a register:

| Projection | Entity | Reading | Register |
|---|---|---|---|
| plan wheel (ring + Āditya band + 12 petals + hub) | **Foundation** | the ṛta-chakra — order administered | operational / institutional |
| front elevation (drop → dome → lotus → waterline) | **Temple** | the axis — order worshipped | sacred (rung 1–2) |
| unringed anāhata lotus (12 petals + bindu) | **Community / devotee** | the heart — order carried within | warm / social (YouTube avatar candidate) |

**What this direction bets:**

1. Entity differentiation by *projection* is deeper than differentiation by
   color or typography: Foundation/Temple/community stop competing for one
   mark because they are one geometry seen differently.
2. The gold god-point recurring in all three (hub / falling drop / lotus
   centre) keeps the family unmistakably one brand — and it is the same
   bindu that sits under the R.
3. The favicon is the axis at its smallest: **the drop over the dome** — an
   ownable 16 px silhouette (a tiny shrine) rather than a generic circled dot.

**Variants shipped:** all three projections in light + dark, temple mono,
drop-over-dome favicon light + dark.

**Risks:** three marks need governance (the sanctity-ladder table IS that
governance — each projection has one register); the elevation must never be
cropped mid-linga (clear-space rule to write in Phase 3); the anāhata lotus
unringed risks reading floral-generic in isolation (mitigated: it never
appears without the family context or the gold centre).
