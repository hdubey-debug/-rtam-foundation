# exp/chakra-seal — hypothesis (Direction A)

**Claim.** The plan-view of the sanctum — the ṛta-chakra — should be the
Foundation's crest. The murti gives the brand a constructible yantra: 12
Āditya medallions on the rim, the 24-petal two-tier lotus, the linga as
hub-bindu. One drawn object replaces "stock font + dot" as the carrier of the
philosophy, and its reduction ladder gives every scale an honest form.

**What this direction bets:**

1. A fine-line engraved yantra can look *modern* (construction-drawing
   discipline, two stroke weights, gold only at god-points) rather than
   clip-art-spiritual.
2. The reduction ladder — full yantra → wheel → bindu-in-ring — solves the
   16 px favicon problem the typographic system never could: at tab size the
   brand IS a circled gold point, which is also its deepest meaning (the
   bindu at the hub).
3. The seal slots into the sanctity ladder at rung 2 (sacred, but linga
   abstracted): certificates, seals, ceremonial print; the wheel serves rung
   3 (operational); the wordmarks remain the public voice.

**Variants shipped:** full yantra (ivory / indigo-gold / mono / nālī-index),
wheel (light / dark), bindu-in-ring favicon (light / dark).

**Nālī study:** the spout that breaks the circle is this direction's ownable
differentiator vs. generic chakra marks — recommended ON for ceremonial
surfaces (it points the wheel at the devotee), OFF where rotational symmetry
must survive tight circle crops (avatars).

**Risks:** medallions vanish below ~48 px (the ladder answers by switching
form, not shrinking); lotus petal drawing must stay engraved (the wheel rung
is the most at risk of reading generic — mitigated with midribs + narrower
petals); gold-line-on-ivory variants are ceremonial-only (2.18:1).
