THE UNSTRUCK SOUND

Anāhata is the sound that no one strikes — so this site is silent by default, and stays silent. If you choose, the ghaṇṭā may sound once; if you choose again, a low tānpurā holds underneath your visit. Your choice is remembered, but sound never starts itself.
