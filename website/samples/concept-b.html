RUNG I — THE POINT
केवलम्

THE LORD ALONE

Before form, before order, before the heart that seeks Him — the single gold point. Transcendent. The same point that sits beneath the Ṛ of ṚTAM.

↓
RUNG II — THE HEART
अनाहत

THE LORD IN YOUR HEART

The twelve-petal lotus of the Anāhata opens around Him. The inner view of the murti: consciousness dwelling where you already are.

↓
RUNG III — THE COSMOS
ऋतस्य पन्थाम्

THE LORD BEARING THE COSMIC ORDER

The same lotus, now seated in the stone rim among the twelve Ādityas — the Ṛta-chakra. The Lord who runs the world is the Lord who dwells in your heart, beyond both.
